WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER

  Install Backplane.exe     Installs Backplane for your Windows account.
  Uninstall Backplane.exe   Removes it again (same as Settings > Apps).
  Guide\                    The Backplane guide. Opens in your browser.
  Legal\                    License and third-party notices.
  SHA256SUMS.txt            Fingerprints to check the download is intact.


INSTALL (about a minute)

  1. Double-click "Install Backplane.exe".
  2. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", choose
     More info > Run anyway. Backplane isn't code-signed yet, so
     SmartScreen doesn't recognise it.
  3. Click through Setup. No administrator password is needed:
     Backplane installs for your Windows account only, in
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Backplane.

  Backplane draws its window with Microsoft Edge WebView2, which
  Windows 11 and most Windows 10 PCs already have. If yours doesn't,
  Setup offers Microsoft's free download; until then Backplane opens
  in your web browser instead.


FIRST STEPS

  - The welcome guide opens on first launch (6 short pages).
  - Try the Bench first. It runs a practice backend with pretend
    accounts, so you can watch a full build, break things on purpose
    and repair them. Nothing real is touched.
  - When you're ready: Accounts > Connect an account, then
    Backends > New backend. Pick one of 25 presets (e-commerce store,
    CRM, booking system, SaaS...) or describe your business in plain
    words.
  - Nothing is ever created until you approve a plan.


UNINSTALL

  Double-click "Uninstall Backplane.exe" here, or open
  Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Backplane > Uninstall.

  - Uninstalling does NOT remove what Backplane built in your
    Cloudflare, Supabase, Stripe, Resend or GitHub accounts. Those keep
    running. To remove them, open the backend in Backplane first and use
    Settings > Tear down.
  - You'll be asked whether to keep your Backplane data (backends,
    history, saved connections) for a later reinstall. It lives in
    %APPDATA%\Backplane.
  - Files you exported stay in Downloads\Backplane exports.


CHECK THE DOWNLOAD (optional)

  Open PowerShell in this folder and run:
    Get-FileHash ".\Install Backplane.exe" -Algorithm SHA256
  The result should match the line in SHA256SUMS.txt.


NEEDS

  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit (x64).
  - An internet connection to build and monitor real backends.
    The Bench works offline.


YOUR DATA

  - Account keys are encrypted with Windows (DPAPI) for your Windows
    account and are only ever sent to the service they belong to.
  - Logs and exports are scrubbed of keys and secrets.
  - The optional Claude assistant (off until you add your own key)
    only receives the plain-English description you type.
